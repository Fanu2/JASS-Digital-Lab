THE PROBLEM

Old software can still contain valuable business logic.

Replacing a legacy system does not have to mean throwing away decades of accumulated data, workflows and domain knowledge. The first step is understanding what exists, what must be preserved and what should be modernized.

01 / Understand

Inventory the system

Identify applications, data files, dependencies, workflows, reports and operational requirements.

02 / Preserve

Protect the data

Work from the existing DBF and related data structures while establishing safe copies and migration paths.

03 / Modernize

Build the next layer

Move appropriate workflows toward Python, SQL, SQLite, PostgreSQL and modern PySide6 desktop interfaces.
