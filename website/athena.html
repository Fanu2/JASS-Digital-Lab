Project Athena

A privacy-first, offline knowledge and research workspace built around documents, retrieval, citations, workflows and local AI.

Explore on GitHub → ← Back to Products
THE IDEA

Build intelligence around your knowledge.

Athena is designed as a local-first personal knowledge system: an engineering platform for research, document intelligence, retrieval and workspace workflows, while keeping the core experience under the user's control.

01 / KNOWLEDGE

Research Workspace

Organize knowledge, documents, sources and workflows into a coherent working environment.

02 / RETRIEVAL

Retrieval Intelligence

Combine query planning, semantic retrieval and application-level orchestration for grounded research.

03 / DOCUMENTS

Document Intelligence

Designed to integrate local document processing, OCR and structured extraction into the knowledge workflow.

04 / LOCAL AI

Local AI Providers

Integrate local model runtimes such as Ollama and LM Studio through provider-oriented architecture.

05 / CITATIONS

Grounded Answers

Retrieval and answer workflows are designed around traceable sources and citation-aware research.

06 / WORKSPACE

Workspace Intelligence

Bring retrieval and context into the user's workspace instead of treating AI as an isolated chat box.

ARCHITECTURE

Integration-first. Local-first.

Athena separates orchestration from infrastructure so local AI, document processing, vector stores and other components can evolve independently.
